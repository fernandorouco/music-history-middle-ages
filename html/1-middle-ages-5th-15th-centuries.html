1. MIDDLE AGES (5th-15th centuries)

Historical Context

The Middle Ages span a long period of time (5th to 15th centuries).  The main characteristics of this period were:

  • Society was divided into closed groups with virtually no social mobility: the nobility and the clergy (the privileged classes) and the peasantry (who worked the land).
  • The political and economic system was feudalism:  based on a relationship of vassalage, whereby the king or the nobles granted land in exchange for loyalty and military protection.
  • The Catholic faith dominated everyday life: the power of the Church dominated daily life, culture and thought of the era.

During this period, musical forms such as Gregorian chant and secular music predominated, and it was during this time that polyphony first emerged.
