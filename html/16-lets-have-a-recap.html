History of Music - Middle Ages

Gregorian Chant and secular music

1.6. Let’s have a recap!

A revision guide!

Monophonic music

  • Religious: Gregorian chant (Latin, monophonic, a cappella, anonymous, religious and free-rhythm).
  • Secular: troubadours and trouvères (solo singing, vernacular languages, varied themes (love, war…), accompanied by instruments, composers with signed works).

The ‘Cantigas’ of Alfonso X the Wise:

  • Written in Galician-Portuguese. Although they refer to the Virgin Mary, they are considered secular music.

Polyphony

  • Ars Antiqua
    • Organum: adding further lines to an existing Gregorian chant.
    • Conductus: the same concept as the Organum but based on newly composed melodies.
    • Motets: compositions for several voices.
  • Ars Nova
    • Greater emphasis on secular music.
    • Chanson: a secular polyphonic genre.
